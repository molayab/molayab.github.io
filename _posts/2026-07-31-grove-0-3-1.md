If Grove kept asking macOS's permission to access your Keychain — the same prompt, showing up again on a later launch instead of staying answered — that's fixed in 0.3.1.

## What was happening

Grove stores your license key in the macOS Keychain rather than a plain settings file, which is the right way to hold something like that. A handful of people were seeing the "Grove wants to use your confidential information stored in..." prompt resurface across launches instead of the one-time approval you'd expect from a normal Keychain item. Nothing was being read or written incorrectly, and no license data was at risk — it was just an avoidable interruption tied to which Keychain the request was landing in.

## What changed

0.3.1 moves that request to the Data Protection Keychain, the storage class Apple's Keychain APIs are steering apps toward now. Same key, same protection, no need to reactivate — just without the repeat prompts for the people who were hitting them.
